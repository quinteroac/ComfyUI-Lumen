# Draft Mode

Draft mode makes small, fast images so you can try many ideas before committing to full size.

Turn it on with the **lightning** button in the imagine bar, or add `--draft` to one prompt. Drafts are 1 megapixel on H3 Image and MOSS Image (Anima is always 1 megapixel).

```prompt
a red fox sitting in tall autumn grass, golden hour wildlife photograph --draft
```

## Bringing a draft to full size

Click **Enhance** on a draft image to regenerate it at the normal size. [Vary](#vary) and [Remix](#remix) on a draft also come back at full size.

> **Tip:** [Conversational mode](#conversational-mode) with voice turns draft mode on, so spoken ideas come back quickly.
