# Remix

Remix redraws an image with a changed prompt, keeping its overall layout.

![The original](cabin.webp)
![Remix (Strong): "a cozy wooden cabin on a tropical beach at sunset, palm trees"](remix.webp)

## How to use it

1. Open an image and choose **Remix › Subtle** or **Strong**.
2. The image's prompt goes into the bar. Change what you want and press Enter.

| Option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Subtle | Small changes: colors, materials, details. The image stays close to the original. |
| Strong | Bigger changes: a new setting, season or subject, with a similar layout. |

Remix also works on drafts, which come back at full size. For a new look on the same structure, see [Retexture](#retexture); to change only part of the image, use the [Editor](#editor).
