# Your Creations

Create lists your jobs, newest first. Each job shows its prompt, its images and what you can do next.

## A job

- Placeholders show how many images are still coming; each image appears as soon as ComfyUI finishes it, with live progress.
- Under the prompt you see the styles a [style code](#style-codes) picked, the pictures that were used and the job's settings.
- **Cancel** stops a running job, **Repeat** runs it again with a new seed, **Use** puts its prompt and pictures back in the bar, and **Repeat in HD** runs it at [HD](#resolution).

## Quick actions

Hover over an image for its shortcuts:

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Heart | Like. Liked images appear in Explore › Likes. |
| Enhance | Regenerates a [draft](#draft-mode) at full size. |
| V·subtle, V·strong | [Vary](#vary). |
| Animate | [Animate](#animate) with low motion. |
| Trash | Moves the image to the trash. |
| Extend (videos) | [Extend](#extend-video) the video. |

Right-click an image for more: describe it, open it in the editor, add it to a folder, or use it as an image prompt.

## The lightbox

Click an image to open it large with every action: Vary, Upscale, Remix, Edit (editor, zoom out, pan), Animate, Use (as image, style or prompt), Describe, and **More** (copy the prompt, seed, job ID or URL, download, search similar, repeat in HD). Arrow keys move between images; Esc closes it.

## Searching

The search box at the top of Create filters your jobs by prompt. Open a folder from the folder button to see only its jobs; what you create while a folder is open is saved in it.
