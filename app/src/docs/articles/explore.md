# Explore

Explore is your gallery: every image and video you made, and the style codes you can use.

## Images and videos

| View | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Recent | Newest first. |
| Random | A shuffled selection. |
| Likes | What you liked with the heart. |

Filter by type (images or videos) and search by the words of the prompt. Click an item to open it with all its actions. Hover for shortcuts: use the image, use the prompt, search similar, like.

**Search similar** finds your creations with the most words in common with that prompt.

## Styles

The **Styles** tab is the [Style Explorer](#style-explorer): style codes with a real preview.
