# The Lumen Interface

The sidebar takes you to every part of Lumen. Most of your time is spent in Create, with the imagine bar at the top.

## Pages

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Explore** | Your gallery of images and videos, and the style explorer. See [Explore](#explore). |
| **Create** | The imagine bar and your jobs, newest first. See [Your Creations](#create-page). |
| **Edit** | The [Editor](#editor) and [Retexture](#retexture). |
| **Personalize** | Profiles that learn your taste. See [Personalization](#personalization). |
| **Moodboards** | Image collections you use as a style. See [Moodboards](#moodboards). |
| **Create styles** | The [Style Creator](#style-creator). |
| **Organize** | Filters, folders, the timeline and the trash. See [Organize](#organize). |
| **ComfyUI** | Models, sizes and your own workflows. See [ComfyUI Settings](#comfyui-settings). |
| **Help** | This documentation. |

## Language and theme

At the bottom of the sidebar you can switch between light and dark mode and pick the language of the interface: Español, English, 日本語 or 中文. Lumen starts in your browser's language and remembers your choice in this browser. This documentation is in English.

## Where your work is kept

Jobs, settings, styles, profiles and folders are saved in ComfyUI's user folder (`user/<user>/lumen/`), so they are there on any device that opens the same ComfyUI. The images and videos themselves are in ComfyUI's `output/lumen/` folder.
