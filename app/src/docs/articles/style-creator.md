# Style Creator

The Style Creator builds a style from your choices: you pick the samples you like, and every few picks Lumen mixes the codes you chose into one `--sref`.

## How to use it

1. Open **Create styles** in the sidebar, or **Open this prompt in the Style creator** from a job.
2. Write a simple prompt for the samples, such as "a lighthouse by the sea", and press **Start**.
3. Each sample is a random style code rendered with your prompt. Click the ones whose style you like, not their content.
4. Every 4 picks, a round renders your prompt with the picked codes mixed (the last 8 at most). Rounds appear on the right with their `--sref`.
5. Click a round's `--sref` to put it in the bar. **End session** takes you back to Create, where the rounds are kept as jobs.

## Tips

- **More samples** loads another 8 when the current ones finish.
- Turn on **Keyboard shortcuts** to pick among the newest samples with the keys 1–9 and 0.
- **Try** runs a single sample's code with your prompt; the heart saves it in Explore › Styles › Likes.

Samples are drafts made with H3 Image, one at a time, always with the same seed; rounds use your normal settings.
