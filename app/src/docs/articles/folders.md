# Folders

Folders group your creations. They can be gathered into folder groups.

## Making folders

- In Organize or Create, open the folder list and type a name with **New folder + Enter**.
- **New group + Enter** creates a group; move folders into it with their ⋯ menu.
- Double-click a group to rename it. Ungrouping keeps its folders.

## Adding images

- Drag images onto a folder.
- Or right-click an image and choose **Add to…**, or use **Add to folder** in the lightbox. **Remove from…** takes it out.

## Working inside a folder

Open a folder in Create to see only its jobs. While it is open, what you create is saved in it. **Leave the folder** goes back to all jobs.

A folder's ⋯ menu renames it, downloads its images, moves it to a group or deletes it. Deleting a folder does not delete the images.
