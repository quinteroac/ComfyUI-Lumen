# Organize

Organize shows all your creations by day, with filters, multiple selection and the trash.

## Filters and search

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| All | Everything except the trash. |
| Images, Videos | One kind only. |
| Likes | Liked items. |
| Upscaled, Edited | Results of Upscale, or of the Editor and other edits. |
| Trash | Items you deleted. |

Search accepts words of the prompt and parameters such as `--ar 16:9` or `HD`. **Save search** keeps a search as a chip for later.

## Selecting

Click items to select them, or select a whole day from its heading. With a selection you can like or unlike, add to a folder or remove from it, download, or move to the trash.

## The timeline

The timeline on the side jumps to a day.

## Trash

Items in the trash do not appear in Create or Explore. Restore them, or **Empty trash** to delete them from Lumen. Emptying the trash does not delete the files from ComfyUI's output folder.

See also [Folders](#folders).
