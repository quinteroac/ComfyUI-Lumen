# Pan

Pan extends an image in one direction, continuing the scene to the left, right, top or bottom.

![The original](zoom-src.webp)
![Pan right](pan-right.webp)

## How to use it

Open an image and choose a direction under **Edit**. Pan adds half the width (left or right) or half the height (up or down), so the image becomes wider or taller.

## How it works

Like [Zoom Out](#zoom-out), Pan puts the image on a larger white canvas and asks the [prompt enhancer](#prompt-basics) to describe what continues in the new area; H3 Image fills it so the seam disappears.

Pan the same image more than once to build panoramas.
