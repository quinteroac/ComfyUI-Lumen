# Style Reference

A style reference is a picture whose look you want, without its content. Lumen takes the rendering, line work and colors and applies them to your prompt.

Drag up to four pictures onto **Style reference** in the imagine bar, or choose **Use › Style** on a result, then write your prompt.

![Style reference](styleref-src.webp)
![an old fisherman mending a net on a pier at sunset](styleref-result.webp)

```prompt
an old fisherman mending a net on a wooden pier at sunset
```

## How it works

Style references run on H3 Image through the [prompt enhancer](#prompt-basics), which looks at each picture and writes the style into the description. The content of the reference does not carry over; describe the subject you want in the prompt.

Moodboards work the same way: their pictures become style references. See [Moodboards](#moodboards).

> **Tip:** For a look you can reuse anywhere, including on Anima and MOSS Image, use a [style code](#style-codes) instead.
